About QAQEST

QAQEST is a free, static practice-exam site for software testing certification study. It offers timed mock exams and untimed practice quizzes organized into modules that mirror well-known certification syllabi topics (Foundation Level, Agile Tester, Advanced Level, and Specialist modules).

Independence & disclaimer

QAQEST is an independent, unofficial study resource created by volunteers and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISTQB® (International Software Testing Qualifications Board) or any national testing board. "ISTQB" and related syllabus names are used strictly as descriptive references to identify the topics a module covers — not as an indication of official partnership. Official sample exams and syllabi are published by ISTQB and its member boards at istqb.org. Passing a QAQEST mock exam does not guarantee success on any official certification exam.

How scoring works

Each module defines its own official-like exam length, time limit, and pass percentage (commonly 65%, matching typical ISTQB exam thresholds). In exam mode, feedback is withheld until you finish so the experience mirrors a real exam; in practice mode, you get instant feedback and an explanation after every question.

Ads are opt-in

QAQEST shows no ads by default. If you'd like to support the project, use the button in the header to switch to "❤️ Support QAQEST with ads" — you can switch back to "🚫 No ads, just studying" at any time. Your choice is saved only in your browser (localStorage) and, when ads are off, no ad slot or ad script is added to the page at all.

Your data

All progress — attempt history, scores, and answers — is stored only in your browser's local storage. QAQEST has no backend, no accounts, and does not transmit your answers anywhere. Clearing your browser data will erase your history.

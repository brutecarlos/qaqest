About QAQuest

QAQuest is a free, static practice-exam site for software testing certification study. It offers timed mock exams and untimed practice quizzes organized into modules that mirror well-known certification syllabi topics (Foundation Level, Agile Tester, Advanced Level, and Specialist modules).

Independence & disclaimer

QAQuest is an independent, unofficial study resource created by volunteers and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISTQB® (International Software Testing Qualifications Board) or any national testing board. "ISTQB" and related syllabus names are used strictly as descriptive references to identify the topics a module covers — not as an indication of official partnership. Official sample exams and syllabi are published by ISTQB and its member boards at istqb.org. Passing a QAQuest mock exam does not guarantee success on any official certification exam.

How scoring works

Each module defines its own official-like exam length, time limit, and pass percentage (commonly 65%, matching typical ISTQB exam thresholds). In exam mode, feedback is withheld until you finish so the experience mirrors a real exam; in practice mode, you get instant feedback and an explanation after every question.

Your data

All progress — attempt history, scores, and answers — is stored only in your browser's local storage. QAQuest has no backend, no accounts, and does not transmit your answers anywhere. Clearing your browser data will erase your history.

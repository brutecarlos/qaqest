Study technique tips

  • Spaced repetition: revisit topics after 1, 3 and 7 days instead of cramming once.
  • Pomodoro: study 25 minutes, break 5 minutes; take a longer break after four rounds.
  • Active recall: answer practice questions before re-reading notes, and always read the explanations.
  • Sleep: a good night before the exam beats a last-minute late-night session.

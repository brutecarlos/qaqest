Frequently Asked Questions

About certification formats, independent practice, browser-based progress, and support.

How many questions, how much time, and what pass mark should I expect?

Formats depend on the certification, syllabus version, and exam provider. The modules currently represented on QAQEST are configured as follows: CTFL 40 questions in 60 minutes; Agile Tester 40 in 60 minutes; Advanced Test Analyst 40 in 90 minutes; Advanced Technical Test Analyst 40 in 90 minutes; Advanced Test Manager 65 in 180 minutes; Test Automation 40 in 60 minutes; and Performance Testing 40 in 60 minutes. These are practice settings, not a claim that every provider currently uses the same format. A pass mark around 65% is typical for the referenced exam style, but check the official body for current rules, language adjustments, and booking details.

Is QAQEST an official exam provider?

No. QAQEST is an independent, unofficial practice resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ISTQB or any national testing board. Syllabus and certification names are descriptive references. The full disclaimer appears on our About page.

How is QAQEST different from the real exam?

QAQEST practice questions are original learning exercises. Their wording, difficulty, coverage, question weighting, time limits, scoring, and interface may differ from an official exam. Practice scores do not predict or guarantee a certification result. Consult the current official syllabus and exam-provider instructions; use practice attempts to identify topics that need more study.

Where is my progress stored?

Attempt history, scores, and answers are stored in localStorage in the browser you use. There is no QAQEST account or server-side progress profile. Different browsers or devices do not automatically share this data. Clearing browser storage can erase history; use the progress export feature where available if you want to keep a backup.

How do I turn ads on or off?

Ads are off by default. Use the header button to opt in to show ads and support QAQEST, or select it again to return to the ad-free state. Your preference is saved in localStorage on that browser. The active quiz screen does not show ads. If browser storage is unavailable, the preference may not persist.

How do I report a question or site issue?

Question review links include a prefilled GitHub issue form. For a general site issue, open the QAQEST repository issue page at https://github.com/brutecarlos/qaqest/issues/new and include the page, browser, steps to reproduce, and expected versus observed behavior. Do not include passwords, personal information, or other sensitive data.

Can I use the guides as the only study material?

The guides summarize selected ideas in original language; they are not a complete syllabus or a substitute for official materials. Check the current learning objectives and candidate information for the exact exam you plan to take. Use the guides to review concepts, then test your understanding with new examples and practice questions.
